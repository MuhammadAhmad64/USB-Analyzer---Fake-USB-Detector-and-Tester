This Privacy Policy describes how USB Analyzer – Fake USB Detector and Tester (“USB Analyzer”, “the app”, “we”, “our” or “us”) accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the app.

This policy was prepared by reviewing the supplied Android project source for package com.tacreations.fakeusbdetectorandtester, version 1.0 (versionCode 1). The reviewed source contains no TACreations account service or app backend, and no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or Firebase SDK dependency. The app's USB inspection and capacity-test processing is performed on your device.

1. Information the app accesses

  • USB hardware descriptors: When a USB device is connected and you grant Android's USB permission, the app reads device information exposed by Android and the USB device. This can include manufacturer and product strings, Vendor ID (VID), Product ID (PID), device revision, serial number (when accessible), interface count and interface descriptors. Some devices or Android versions may not expose every field.
  • USB connection information: The app observes USB attach/detach events and the list of connected USB devices so it can show connection status and identify a device for inspection.
  • Storage-volume information: During a capacity test, the app checks whether the selected mounted volume is removable and obtains available-space information where Android permits it.
  • Capacity-test data: If you choose a test, the app creates a temporary file named similar to usb_analyzer_test_*.bin in the folder you select, writes generated test data, reads the data back and compares SHA-256 checksums. This is intended to test only the selected data range. The app does not intentionally read or upload your existing personal files.
  • Serial-number clipboard action: If you tap the serial-number copy action, the serial number is placed on the Android clipboard at your request. Clipboard display, access and retention are controlled by Android and your device.
  • Local preference: The app stores a local preference recording whether you accepted the first-launch disclaimer. The reviewed source does not create an account or store a user profile.

2. Capacity testing, temporary files and your data

Capacity testing is optional and begins only after you select a test mode, choose a storage folder through Android's system folder picker and proceed. The app is designed to create and delete only its own temporary test file; it does not intentionally delete or overwrite your existing files.

  • Always back up important data before running any capacity test.
  • Keep the USB drive or card reader connected and avoid using the selected storage location while the test is running.
  • The app attempts to delete its temporary test file after completion, cancellation or an error.
  • If the device is disconnected, power is lost, or the file system reports an error, cleanup may not be confirmed. The app reports the cleanup status. If a file remains, inspect the selected USB folder and remove only the temporary file created by USB Analyzer.
  • A successful result applies only to the amount actually written and read back. It does not prove that untested capacity is functional or that the product is genuine.

Test data and SHA-256 calculations are processed locally. The reviewed source does not send the temporary test file, its contents, or the test result to a TACreations server.

3. Manufacturer websites and serial-number autofill

The app provides manufacturer information and support pages for brands including SanDisk, ADATA, Kingston, Transcend, Samsung and Lexar. These pages are loaded inside the app using Android WebView over HTTPS.

For ADATA and Transcend pages, when a serial number is available from the connected USB device, the app attempts to place that value into a recognizable serial-number input field on the loaded page. This is a convenience feature only: it does not submit the form automatically. You remain responsible for reviewing the page and deciding whether to submit the serial number or any other information.

When you visit or submit information to a manufacturer website, that manufacturer may receive information such as your IP address, browser/device information, cookies, the serial number or other details you choose to submit. The manufacturer may process this information under its own privacy policy. TACreations does not control those websites and does not receive the information you submit to them merely because the page is displayed in the app.

The WebView has JavaScript and DOM storage enabled because some manufacturer portals require them. File access, content access, mixed HTTP/HTTPS content and automatic pop-up windows are disabled in the reviewed WebView configuration. Navigation to non-HTTPS schemes is blocked, and WebView SSL certificate errors are cancelled.

4. Information shared with third parties

  • Manufacturer website operators: Receive network requests when you open their pages. They receive serial numbers or other form data only if the page is populated and you choose to submit that form.
  • Google Play services: The app includes Google Play In-App Updates and In-App Review libraries. The app may contact Google Play services to check for an available app update or request the in-app review flow. Google may process app, installation, device or service-related information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The reviewed project does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, Firebase SDK or TACreations-operated server integration. We do not sell personal information. This statement describes the supplied source project; behavior of Android, Google Play services and third-party websites is governed by their respective providers.

5. Permissions and Android system interfaces

  • Internet permission: Used for HTTPS manufacturer pages and Google Play update/review services.
  • USB host feature and Android USB permission: Used to detect and inspect connected USB hardware. Android displays and controls the USB permission prompt.
  • Android Storage Access Framework folder picker: Used so you can select the removable storage folder for a capacity test. The app does not request broad shared-storage read/write permissions in the reviewed manifest.

The reviewed app manifest declares the Internet permission and optional USB host feature. It does not declare camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, phone or call-log permissions.

6. Data storage, retention and backup

The reviewed app stores the disclaimer-acceptance preference locally using Android SharedPreferences. USB descriptors and capacity-test data are processed for the feature you invoke and are not uploaded to a TACreations account or server by the reviewed source.

The app manifest has Android backup enabled and references Android backup/data-extraction rule files. The supplied rule files contain no explicit include or exclude entries. Therefore, whether eligible app-local data is included in device backup or device transfer can depend on Android version, device settings and system backup behavior. Do not treat the local preference as a secure secret.

Clipboard contents are managed by Android. Manufacturer websites and Google Play services apply their own retention and deletion practices.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can deny USB permission; USB inspection features may then be unavailable.
  • You can choose not to open manufacturer pages or submit any information to them.
  • You can cancel a capacity test. The app will report the amount written/read back and whether it could confirm deletion of its temporary file.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall USB Analyzer through Android settings. This removes app-local data managed by Android, subject to system backup behavior.
  • For information submitted directly to a manufacturer or processed by Google Play, contact that provider or use its privacy controls.

There is no USB Analyzer user account and no in-app account-deletion workflow in the reviewed source.

8. Security

USB inspection and capacity-test operations are performed locally. Manufacturer web pages are restricted to HTTPS navigation, and WebView certificate errors are cancelled. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your Android operating system and Android System WebView updated, and use official manufacturer resources.

9. Children

USB Analyzer is a general-purpose hardware utility and is not designed specifically for children. The reviewed version does not provide a user account or knowingly collect personal information from children through a TACreations backend.
